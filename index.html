About Me

I am an assistant professor of strategy and policy at the United States Naval War College. I study modern Chinese history and am currently working on a book about the bureaucratic development of the institutions through which the Chinese state engages with the world and the professional identities of experts working within those institutions. I received a PhD in history from U.C. Berkeley in 2024 and previously held fellowships at Harvard University's Fairbank Center for Chinese Studies, the Harvard Kennedy School's Belfer Center, and the Columbia-Harvard China & the World Program. My research has been published in the International History Review and Cold War History. My writing on China has also appeared in publications including the Washington Post, Foreign Policy, the Los Angeles Review of Books, and History Today.

Publications

  • "China Marching with India": India’s Cold War advocacy for the People’s Republic of China at the United Nations, 1949–1971
